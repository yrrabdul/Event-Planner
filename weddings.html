Questions

Simple answers before you book.

How early should we start?

For larger weddings, starting 8–16 weeks ahead gives more room for venue and vendor choices. We can also support shorter timelines where availability allows.

Can you work with vendors we already booked?

Yes. We can coordinate existing vendors and only source the remaining services.

Can we book only event-day coordination?

Yes. We can scope a coordination-only service once we understand what is already planned.
